# Grading

Each answer is pass or fail against the key, never half.

- **Pass:** right, specific and from memory: the claim, the file, the reason.
- **Fail:**
  - vague, or right only in part
  - right but guessed, the person says so, or they can't give the reason when asked
  - looked up during the exam
  - "I don't know". That is an honest fail; note it as such.

## Dimensions

For a part with a spec, each dimension needs at least two questions or tasks:

| Dimension | Shown by |
|---|---|
| Intent | why it exists, in their own words, matching the Purpose |
| Contract | the promise, and its edges |
| Claims | which tests prove what; predicting their results |
| Seams | what crosses each seam, and what happens when the other side fails |
| Decisions | why this design, what was rejected, what it costs |
| Failure modes | the drill and the what-if questions |
| Practice | bug hunt, silent bug and a change |

For a topic without a spec, the dimensions are concept, mechanism, why, failure modes and practice. Write no EXAMINED line for it.

A dimension passes when every question in it passes. The exam passes when every dimension passes. There is no curve and no overall score.
