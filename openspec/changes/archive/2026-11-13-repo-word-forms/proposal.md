## Why
check never reports a word docs/glossary.md defines, but compared only the exact word. wrong-term counts a plural as the same word, so a repo defining `PRs` still got `PRs` reported as the plural of the Not term `PR`.

- **glossary**: a word docs/glossary.md defines protects its singular and its plural, in the forms wrong-term counts: s, es, and y to ies.
