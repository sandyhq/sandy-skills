## Why

A word count the owner cannot predict is no limit. He needs to know what counts, and that finished work in the archive never blocks a release.

## Your decisions

None open.

## What Changes

- **cli** counts as a word each token with a letter or digit; Markdown marks like `-` and `##` count as no word.
- **Not counted**: fenced code, HTML comments, TEST lines and the claim text of Prove tasks.
- **Open changes only**: check never measures `openspec/changes/archive/` or `openspec/specs/`.

## Not in this change

- The thresholds.
- Vague words.
