## Why
A repo has words of its own domain. A word of the standard's Not lines can be a right word there, for example `PR` as a purchase request in a shop.

## What Changes
- Each repo writes the words of its own domain in `docs/glossary.md`.
- A word that `docs/glossary.md` defines is never a wrong term.
